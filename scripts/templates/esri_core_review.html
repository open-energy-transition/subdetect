Core Label Review

Each card is one substation label from the Sentinel-2 training set. The rewrite keeps only the part of the OSM polygon where a DINOv2 probe on Esri imagery (0.3–2 m) sees switchyard equipment. The rest of the polygon is ignored in training rather than taught as background.

Both retraining studies came out neutral: Sentinel-1 fused AUC +0.018 over three seeds, Sentinel-2 −0.017 over two seeds, with both S2 seeds below control. This sheet is for checking the labels by eye. Was the trimming right, and where does it go wrong?

QC sample (60): the frozen G0.4 set, skewed toward large sites, with Claude's grades shown. Random sample (40): drawn fresh from the training set (24 TorchGeo, 8 India, 8 Pakistan). It excludes every site used for tuning, QC or probe training.
